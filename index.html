About

LLM Journal Club is a community of students, researchers, and techies at Sharif and beyond. Every week, we meet online to discuss the latest research, tools, applications, and trends in Large Language Models.

Format: Short presentations + in-depth discussions.
Focus: Recent LLM papers, benchmarks, fine-tuning, agent frameworks, tool use, AI safety, etc.

🗓 Usually Wednesdays. Got a topic or want to present? Ping us on Telegram!
