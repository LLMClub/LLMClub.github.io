About Our Journal Club

Welcome to the LLM Journal Club at Sharif University of Technology, a premier academic forum dedicated to exploring the rapidly evolving landscape of Large Language Models and Artificial Intelligence. Our weekly sessions serve as a vital bridge between cutting-edge research and practical applications, bringing together distinguished researchers, industry experts, and passionate graduate students.

Since its inception, our journal club has become a cornerstone of AI research discussion at Sharif University, fostering intellectual discourse on the most impactful developments in natural language processing, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. Each session features in-depth presentations on recent breakthroughs, methodological advances, and emerging trends that are shaping the future of AI.

Our diverse lineup of speakers includes renowned professors, leading researchers from top-tier institutions, and innovative PhD candidates who present their latest findings and insights. These sessions not only disseminate knowledge but also encourage critical thinking, collaborative research, and the development of new ideas that push the boundaries of what's possible in AI.
