Why join LLM Journal Club?
Language models are reshaping how we understand and build intelligent systems. LLM Journal Club is a space to connect, stay updated, and go beyond the headlines — hear from real practitioners, debate new ideas, and get inspired.
  • Get sharp on the latest LLM models, techniques, and open challenges
  • Connect with peers from Sharif and the wider AI community, every week
  • Low commitment, high value: just drop in, learn, and join the discussion!
Whether you’re a student, researcher, or a curious autodidact, we’d love to have you.
